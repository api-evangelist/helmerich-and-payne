## Technologies
- [StallAssist® technology](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/stallassist-technology): Advanced control that actively detects, alerts and  mitigates downhole motor stalls reduce the risk of  downhole tool damage\.
- [FlexB2D® 2\.0 technology](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/flexb2d-2-0-technology): Fully integrated automated process control to optimize and replicate the process of starting a stand after a connection and engaging the bit with the formation\.
- [Autodriller Pro Control System](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/autodriller-pro-control-system): A fully integrated advanced control system that prioritizes consistent bit engagement to maximize ROP and reduce axial vibrations \(bit bounce\) 
- [Power Management](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/power-management): Optimizes energy use across the rig to reduce fuel consumption, emissions and operational costs\.
- [FlexOscillator® technology](https://www.hpinc.com/technologies/flexoscillator-technology): Enhances directional drilling performance by reducing axial friction to improve slide efficiency in wellbores\.
